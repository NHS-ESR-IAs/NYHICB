Quick Login Checklist

  1. Open ESR using the ICB-approved access route.
  2. Insert the Smartcard associated with your ESR record and select Log in with my Care Identity (or sign in via configured internet access).
  3. Once on the ESR Portal, open the left navigation menu and select My Role → Manager.
Smartcard Locked? Self-Service Unlock Procedure

If your Smartcard is locked, you can unlock it via the NHS Care Identity Service self-service portal:

  1. Insert your Smartcard and open the Care Identity Self-Service Unlock portal.
  2. Choose email or mobile delivery for your one-time security code.
  3. Follow the prompts to set and confirm a new PIN (always complete this privately off shared screens).

Key Steps When Recording Sickness

  1. Select Absence Type & Reason: Choose Sickness and the appropriate medical/absence reason from the lookup list.
  2. Enter Start Date (Required): You can log an open-ended sickness absence with only a Start Date and add the End Date once the colleague returns.
  3. Check Duration & Hours Lost: After clicking Calculate Duration, verify that Hours Lost / Working Days Lost reflect the colleague's actual shift/work pattern.
  4. Supporting Details: Record whether the absence is Work Related, Third Party, or Disability Related, and log the Return to Work Discussion Date and Occupational Health Referral Date where applicable.

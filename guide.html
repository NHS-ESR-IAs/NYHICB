Part 1

Logging in to ESR & Smartcards

  1. Open ESR using the ICB-approved route (the ESR portal link on your desktop or intranet).
  2. Put in the Smartcard linked to your ESR record.
  3. Choose Log in with my Care Identity. This is the lower box on the login screen.
  4. In the portal, open the menu and select My Role, then Manager.
Can't see "Manager"? Ask the ICB ESR team to check your access. See Get help.
My Smartcard is locked

You can unlock it yourself. Put the card in, open the Care Identity self-service unlock page, choose email or text for your security code, then set a new PIN. Do this privately, not on a shared screen.

Logging in from home (without a Smartcard)

If you have upgraded internet access, use Manage Internet Access and enter the one-time code sent to your registered mobile number.

Part 4

Your team

Organisation Chart – user manual ↗
Check your line reports are correct If someone is missing or shouldn't be there, approvals and notifications will go to the wrong person.
  1. On your dashboard, select Launch Organisation Chart.
  2. Check everyone who reports to you is shown.
  3. Use Actions on a person's card to make changes to their record.
  4. If someone is wrong, assign them to the correct manager.

Part 6

Recording sickness

Recording sickness – user manual ↗
  1. Choose the employee, then Create Absence.
  2. Set Absence Type to Sickness and pick an Absence Reason.
  3. Enter the Start Date. This is required. You can add the end date when they come back.
  4. Select Calculate Duration, then check the hours or days lost.
  5. Fill in any other details that apply, such as work-related, return to work discussion date or occupational health referral.
  6. Select Next to review and submit.
Check the duration ESR counts every calendar day between the start and end dates. You may need to change it to match the person's working pattern, for example to leave out weekends.
